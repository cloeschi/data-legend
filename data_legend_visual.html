DATA LEGEND

Profile Metrics

Pageviews
Number of page views of your tool profile (includes all profile pages).
Sessions
Visit sessions on your tool profile. A session includes all interactions within a specific timeframe.
Unique Users
Individual people who visited your profile. Each person counted once (opt-in users only).
Avg. Session Duration
Average time spent per visit in minutes. Measures active interaction (scrolling, reading, clicking).
AI Traffic in %
Percentage of traffic from AI-powered sources (ChatGPT, Perplexity).
Profile Clickouts
Total clicks from your profile to your tool website (excluding LinkedIn & X).
CTR (Profile to Website)
Click-Through Rate: (Clicks ÷ Sessions) × 100.
Website Clickouts
Clicks on the main website button.
Second CTA Contact Clicks
Clicks on secondary call-to-action buttons.
Pricing Clickout
Clicks on pricing information.
LinkedIn/X Clickout
Clicks on social media profiles.
ContentHub Mentions
Number of mentions in OMR Content Hub articles (tool must be listed as software).
Top Traffic Sources
Origins of traffic (Google, LinkedIn, direct, etc.).
PDP (Product Detail Page)
Detail pages of other tools on OMR Reviews.
CDP (Category Detail Page)
Category overview pages on OMR Reviews.

Review Metrics

Total Reviews
Total number of all reviews received.
New Reviews
Number of new reviews in selected time period (with % change).
Category Slug
Number of reviews broken down by categories.
NPS (Net Promoter Score)
Customer satisfaction score measuring likelihood to recommend.
OMR Score
Average overall rating of your tool (scale 0-10).
Avg. Ease of Use
Average rating of user-friendliness (0-10).
Avg. Ease of Setup
Average rating of setup simplicity (0-10).
Avg. Requirement Score
Average rating of how well the tool meets requirements (0-10).
Avg. Customer Support
Average rating of customer support (0-10).
Monthly Reviews
Visualization of review distribution across months.
Category Reviews
Number of reviews per category/use case.
Category Check Rank
Your position within different categories. CDP = Category page ranking. Lower rank = better position.
Is Top-Rated
Status indicating whether your tool is marked as "Top-Rated" (Yes/No).

Benchmark Metrics

Vendor
Your individual performance metrics.
Category Avg.
Average values of all tools in your main category.
Top 3 Avg.
Average values of the three highest-ranked tools in your category.
Comparison Metrics
Includes: Pageviews • Review Count • PDP Clickouts • Avg. Time on Site
Tool Traffic Over Time
Monthly breakdown of all traffic metrics over selected timeframe showing trends and seasonal patterns.
Opt-in
Users who accepted cookie tracking, allowing accurate unique user measurement.
